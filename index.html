How Panda Pay Works

Simple for passengers. Powerful for drivers & SACCOs.

1
Board the Matatu

Find the Panda Pay QR code or till number displayed inside the vehicle.

2
Pay Instantly

Scan the QR or enter the till number and pay using M-Pesa or Airtel Money.

3
Ride Confirmed

Driver sees payment instantly. You get a digital receipt via SMS.
